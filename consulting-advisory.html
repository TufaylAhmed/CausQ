Questions, answered

Before you reach out.

What is a wireless site survey, and do I need one?

A site survey measures how wireless signal actually behaves in your space, using predictive modelling, passive scans and active testing. If you are rolling out Wi-Fi 6 or 7, opening a new site, or chasing dead spots, a survey turns guesswork into an access-point plan and a channel design you can deploy with confidence.

How do you run an upgrade or migration without an outage?

We rehearse the change before the maintenance window, script a rollback for every step, and stay on the bridge until the cutover is validated and signed off. Where it earns its keep we automate the change with infrastructure-as-code so it is repeatable and reversible.

Are you tied to specific vendors?

No. We are vendor-agnostic and there is no product quota behind our advice. We work across Cisco, Juniper, Arista, Palo Alto and others, and we recommend what serves the outcome, not a reseller target.

Can you just advise, or do you also deliver the work?

Both. You can take a fixed-scope assessment or design and run it yourself, or have the same engineers who wrote the plan deliver the upgrade, migration or build, and keep it running afterwards.

How quickly can you start, and what does a first engagement look like?

Most engagements open with a short briefing or a fixed-scope survey or assessment, which we can usually schedule within days. You get findings, risks and a prioritised plan, then decide how far to take it.

Do you work outside the US and EMEA?

Our delivery hubs are in the US and EMEA with follow-the-sun coverage, and we support global enterprises operating across those regions. If your footprint extends further, tell us and we will be straight about what we can cover.
