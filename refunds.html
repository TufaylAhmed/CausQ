Cancellation & Refund Policy

How we bill for our work, and how cancellations and refunds are handled.

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Note. Client engagements are governed by your separately signed proposal or statement of work. This policy supplements that agreement, where the two differ, the signed agreement controls.

This policy explains how CausQ charges for its services and how cancellations and refunds work. It applies to payments made to CausQ for consulting, advisory and managed services, including any payment collected online through this site.

Services, not goods

CausQ provides professional services. We do not sell or ship physical products, so return and replacement of goods do not apply. Where an engagement produces a deliverable, such as a report, design or configuration, that deliverable is governed by your engagement agreement rather than by a returns process.

Your engagement agreement

Every engagement is governed by a signed proposal or statement of work that sets out the scope, fees, schedule and milestones. Where this policy and your signed agreement differ, the signed agreement controls.

Payment terms

  • Fees are quoted and charged in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹) unless your agreement states a different currency. International clients may be billed in US Dollars (USD) by prior arrangement.
  • We may collect an advance deposit and milestone, or partial, payments before or during an engagement, as set out in your statement of work. A typical engagement begins with a deposit of 30 to 50 percent of the fee.
  • Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date (net 30) unless stated otherwise. Recurring or managed-service fees are billed monthly in advance.
  • Accepted methods: secure online payment (card, net banking or UPI) through our payment provider, or bank transfer.
  • Online payments are processed over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection by our payment provider. CausQ does not store your full card details.

Cancelling an engagement

  • Either party may cancel an engagement in writing, giving the notice period set out in the statement of work (typically 30 days).
  • On cancellation you remain responsible for fees for work performed and for committed costs up to the effective cancellation date.
  • For a scheduled briefing or workshop, please give at least 48 hours’ notice to reschedule without charge. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice may be charged in full.

Refunds

  • Amounts paid in advance for work not yet performed are refundable, less any non-refundable deposit and committed costs.
  • Fees for services already delivered are non-refundable.
  • A deposit becomes non-refundable once work on the engagement has begun, unless your statement of work says otherwise.
  • To request a refund, email hello@causq.com with your invoice number and the reason. We review requests within 7 business days.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The time for funds to appear depends on your bank or card issuer.

Failed or duplicate payments

If you are charged more than once, or a payment fails but your account is debited, contact us and we will refund the duplicate or failed amount to the original source within 5 to 7 business days.

Chargebacks and disputes

If something looks wrong, please contact us first. Most issues are resolved quickly. Raising a chargeback with your bank before contacting us may delay resolution.

Transaction currency

All prices shown and all transactions processed through this site and our invoices are in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹), unless your agreement specifies a different currency.
