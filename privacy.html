Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information, across the globe.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Template notice. This policy is a starting point. Before publishing, have it reviewed by qualified privacy counsel and tailored to your actual data practices, entities and the jurisdictions you operate in (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA).

CausQ (“CausQ”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Who we are

CausQ is a network and security consultancy operating worldwide. For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, CausQ is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our contact details are at the end of this policy.

What we collect

  • Information you give us, such as your name, work email, company, role and the contents of enquiries or applications you submit through our forms.
  • Information collected automatically, limited technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and understand usage.
  • Recruitment information, if you apply for a role, the details and links you provide.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request.
  • To consider your application for employment.
  • To send you communications you’ve asked for, such as our newsletter (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • To operate, secure and improve our website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR applies, we rely on your consent (e.g. newsletter), the performance of a contract or steps taken at your request, our legitimate interests (e.g. responding to business enquiries and securing our site), and compliance with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf under appropriate contracts, and where required by law. International transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise any right, contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. For more on how we secure our systems, see our Security page.

Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details.
