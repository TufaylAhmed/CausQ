❯  Zero trust fabric[3/10]
Never trust, always verify

Zero trust, engineered into the network.

Every session authenticated, every device checked, every flow scoped to least privilege. Not a bolt-on product: an access fabric wired through identity, segment and policy.

NEVER TRUST.
ALWAYS VERIFY. LEAST PRIVILEGE BY DEFAULT.
IDENTITY
SESSION VERIFIED · re-checked continuously
policy "finance-erp" {
  allow  = identity.mfa
        && device.compliant
  scope  = "least-privilege"
  verify = "continuous"
}

Identity-first access

Access follows the person and the device posture, not the IP address. SSO, MFA and conditional policy in front of every application.

Microsegmentation

The network is carved into small, policy-bound segments, so one compromised host is a contained event, not a lateral-movement runway.

Continuous verification

Trust decays. Sessions are re-evaluated as posture, location and behaviour change, and revoked the moment they drift.

❯  FAQ[10/10]
01Are you really vendor-agnostic?

Yes. We carry deep certifications across Cisco, Juniper, Arista, Palo Alto Networks, NVIDIA and the hyperscalers, but no resale targets. The architecture is chosen for your requirements, and we put the reasoning in writing.

02How senior is the team, actually?

The engineers who scope your program deliver your program. Our bench is built from people who have run enterprise estates themselves, and we keep teams small enough that you know everyone on them.

03Do you work globally?

Yes. Delivery is remote-first with on-site presence where the work needs hands on hardware, and managed operations follow the sun, 24/7.

04How does an engagement start?

With a free briefing. If there’s a fit, the usual next step is a fixed-scope assessment that produces a roadmap you own, whether or not we deliver it.

05Will we be locked in?

No. Everything we build is documented, automated and handed over. Lock-in is a design flaw, and we treat it as one.

06Can you work alongside our existing partners?

Routinely. We integrate with incumbent MSPs, carriers and resellers, and we’re comfortable being the smallest, most senior team in the room.
