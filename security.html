Security at CausQ

Security is our craft. We hold our own house to the standard we set for clients.

We help enterprises defend themselves, so it would be strange not to take our own security seriously. This page summarises how we protect our systems and how to report a problem responsibly.

How we protect our systems

  • Zero Trust by default. Identity-first access, least privilege, and continuous verification across our own estate.
  • Encryption everywhere. Data encrypted in transit and at rest, with an eye on crypto-agility and post-quantum readiness.
  • Hardened, monitored infrastructure. Secure configuration, logging and detection across the systems we run.
  • Least-data principle. We collect only what we need and retain it only as long as necessary, see our Privacy Policy.
  • People and process. Background-appropriate access, security training, and incident response procedures we actually rehearse.

Responsible disclosure

If you believe you’ve found a security vulnerability in our website or systems, we want to hear from you. Please email security@causq.com with enough detail to reproduce the issue. We ask that you:

  • Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.
  • Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, or disruption of our services while testing.
  • Only interact with accounts you own or have explicit permission to access.

We will acknowledge legitimate reports, keep you informed, and credit researchers who wish to be recognised. We do not pursue legal action against researchers who follow this policy in good faith.

For urgent or sensitive reports, request our PGP key at security@causq.com. This page describes intended practice and should be aligned with your actual controls and any certifications (e.g. SOC 2, ISO 27001) before publishing.

Compliance

We align our practices to recognised frameworks and to the regulatory expectations of the markets we serve across the US and EMEA. Certification details and attestations are available to clients on request.
