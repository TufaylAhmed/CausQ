Compliance and data protection at CausQ

We hold our own house to the standard we set for clients. This page sets out the frameworks we align to, globally and across the GCC, and how to request the detail.

CausQ helps technology and security leaders meet regulatory expectations, so we map our own controls to the same recognised frameworks. We describe how we align below. For how we collect, use and protect personal data, see our Privacy Policy, and for how we run our systems, see our Security page.

Global frameworks we align to

  • GDPR and UK GDPR. Where these apply, CausQ acts as data controller for the personal data it holds and supports data-subject rights, lawful basis, and international transfers under Standard Contractual Clauses. The mechanics live in our Privacy Policy.
  • CCPA and CPRA. We respect consumer privacy rights for residents of California and other jurisdictions with comparable rules.
  • ISO/IEC 27001. Our information-security management practices are designed to align to the ISO 27001 control set: risk assessment, access control, supplier management, and continual improvement.
  • SOC 2. We operate to the SOC 2 trust services criteria of security, availability and confidentiality across the systems we run.
  • DORA and NIS2. For clients in scope of EU operational-resilience and network-security rules, we align our engagements and our own posture to these expectations.

GCC and Saudi regulatory alignment

For clients operating in the Gulf, we align engagements and our own controls to the relevant national cybersecurity and data-protection frameworks where you do business.

  • SAMA Cyber Security Framework (Saudi Arabia). We align to the Saudi Central Bank framework that governs cyber resilience for the Kingdom’s financial sector, covering governance, risk management, third-party risk, and incident response.
  • NCA ECC (Saudi Arabia). We map controls to the National Cybersecurity Authority Essential Cybersecurity Controls, and to its cloud and critical-systems controls where they apply.
  • Saudi PDPL. We support the Personal Data Protection Law, including data-residency and cross-border transfer expectations for personal data of individuals in the Kingdom.
  • UAE: NESA / SIA and the UAE PDPL. We align to the UAE Information Assurance standards and to the federal Personal Data Protection Law.
  • Wider GCC. We work to the national frameworks and data-protection laws of Qatar, Bahrain, Oman and Kuwait as engagements require.

How we operate

  • Data residency. Where regulation or contract requires it, we keep data in-region and design engagements around residency and sovereignty needs.
  • Vetted sub-processors. We use a limited set of reputable providers under appropriate data processing terms, and keep the list available to clients.
  • Encryption everywhere. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with attention to crypto-agility and post-quantum readiness.
  • Least privilege and least data. Identity-first access, continuous verification, and collecting only what we need for only as long as we need it.
  • Logging and audit. Monitored, hardened infrastructure with audit trails to support investigation and attestation.

Attestations and requests

Certification details, control mappings, our sub-processor list, and a data processing agreement are available to clients on request, under NDA where appropriate. Email privacy@causq.com for compliance documentation, or security@causq.com for security questionnaires and disclosures.
