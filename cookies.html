Cookie Policy

What cookies we use, and how to control them.

Last updated: 1 January 2026

Template notice. Update this list to match the cookies your site actually sets once analytics or marketing tools are added, and align it with your consent banner and local law (e.g. ePrivacy / GDPR).

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use a minimal set to keep the site working and to understand, in aggregate, how it is used.

Categories we use

  • Strictly necessary, required for the site to function and to keep it secure. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics (optional), help us understand usage so we can improve the site. Set only with your consent.
  • Preferences (optional), remember choices such as region, where applicable.

We currently do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and where a consent banner is shown you can change your choices at any time. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.
